# ❓ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common questions and answers.

---

## 🔐 Authentication

**Q: How do I reset my password?**
A: Click "Forgot Password" on login page. Check email for reset link.

**Q: Can I use Google to login?**
A: Yes, click "Login with Google" button. Complete profile on first login.

**Q: What if I don't receive the reset email?**
A: Check spam folder. Contact admin if still not received.

---

## 👥 Team Management

**Q: How do I create a team?**
A: Go to "Tim Saya" → Click "Buat Tim Baru" → Fill form → Submit.

**Q: Can I edit my team after creating it?**
A: Yes, if team status is "Pending". Click "Edit" button.

**Q: What if my team is rejected?**
A: Check rejection reason. Fix composition. Create new team.

**Q: How many members can be in a team?**
A: Depends on use case. Usually 4-5 members.

**Q: Can I remove a member from my team?**
A: Yes, if team status is "Pending". Click delete button.

---

## 📧 Invitations

**Q: I received an invitation. What should I do?**
A: Go to "Tim Saya". Click "Accept" or "Reject".

**Q: What happens if I accept an invitation?**
A: You'll be added to the team. Can see team details.

**Q: What happens if I reject an invitation?**
A: You won't be added. Team leader notified.

**Q: Can I undo my acceptance?**
A: Contact admin to remove from team.

---

## 📄 Documents

**Q: Where can I find capstone guidelines?**
A: Go to "Dokumen" page. Search or filter by category.

**Q: Can I download documents?**
A: Yes, click "Download" button on any document.

**Q: Are documents always available?**
A: Yes, unless admin removes them.

---

## 📅 Timeline

**Q: Where can I see project deadlines?**
A: Go to "Timeline" page. Check for red "Overdue" badges.

**Q: When is team registration deadline?**
A: Check Timeline page for exact date.

**Q: Can I see past events?**
A: Yes, Timeline shows all events including completed ones.

---

## 📋 Worksheet

**Q: When do I submit worksheets?**
A: During active worksheet period. Check Timeline for dates.

**Q: What should I include in worksheet?**
A: Describe activities, include evidence files, be specific.

**Q: Can I edit after submitting?**
A: No, submission is final. Contact admin if needed.

**Q: What if I submit late?**
A: Still accepted but marked as late. May affect grade.

---

## 📸 Profile

**Q: How do I upload a profile picture?**
A: Go to Profile → Click "Choose Photo" → Select image.

**Q: What image formats are supported?**
A: JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP. Max 5MB.

**Q: Can I delete my profile picture?**
A: Yes, click "Delete Photo" button.

---

## 🆘 Technical Issues

**Q: The app won't load. What should I do?**
A: Hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R). Clear cache. Try different browser.

**Q: API calls are failing. What's wrong?**
A: Check internet connection. Verify backend is running. Check browser console.

**Q: I can't login. What should I do?**
A: Check email and password. Try "Login with Google". Reset password.

**Q: My team creation failed. Why?**
A: Check all members are valid. Verify composition matches use case.

---

## 📞 Support

**Q: Who do I contact for help?**
A: Check Support Channels page. Email admin or use chat.

**Q: How long does it take to get support?**
A: Usually within 24 hours. Critical issues: 1 hour.

**Q: Can I report a bug?**
A: Yes, use Bug Report Template. Include details and steps to reproduce.
